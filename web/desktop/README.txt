Bird Finder — Desktop
=====================

Identify birds by their calls, on your PC. Nothing to install.

1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. Double-click "Start Bird Finder.bat".
3. Your browser opens Bird Finder. Keep the small black window open while you use it;
   close it when you're done.

Use Chrome or Edge for best results. Allow microphone access when asked to listen live,
or drop audio files (MP3, AAC/M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG) into "Identify a file".

If Windows SmartScreen warns about the .bat file, click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

Audio is analysed on this PC and never uploaded. Photos, maps and weather need an
internet connection.
